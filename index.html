The Five-Leak Audit

My audit system for DTC email. I check five leaks: a welcome flow that does not sell the second visit, a cart flow that gives up too early, no real post-purchase follow-up, campaigns sent to everyone at once, and a list that has gone quiet with no win-back. Most brands I looked at were leaking in at least two spots.

Hi, I am Addison. I help DTC brands get more from the list they already have. Whether you say it Klah-vee-oh or Klay-vee-oh, I promise your emails will sound a lot better once I have had a look at them.

I am a freelance email copywriter focused on Shopify brands in Klaviyo. I came to this by auditing - I looked at 66 brands, reviewed more than 40 flows, and kept seeing the same gaps. That turned into the Five-Leak Audit, and into the way I now write flows: start with what is leaking, fix that first, and keep the copy warm and clear.

I am based in Tacoma and study Mechanical Engineering at UW Tacoma. That background helps more than you would think - I like systems, I test things, and I care about how the pieces fit together. When you hire me, you work with me directly. I reply myself, I write myself, and I build in Klaviyo myself.
