01 / The named enemy

Five leaks. One broken path to order two.

Every leak gets a name, a priority, and a next fix. No vague advice. No bloated campaign calendar. Only the broken handoffs that cost the next order.

  1. 01The Signup Leak

    The form asks for an email without giving the shopper a reason to start the relationship.

  2. 02The Welcome Leak

    The flow leads with a discount, then skips the product education and proof that make the first order feel inevitable.

  3. 03The Checkout Leak

    The reminder stops at one email instead of answering the hesitation that stopped the purchase.

  4. 04The Second-Order Leak

    Delivery ends the conversation before education, replenishment, or a useful cross-sell can earn the return.

  5. 05The Win-Back Leak

    Lapsed customers hear another generic offer instead of a reason that fits what they bought and why they left.

Every week these leaks stay open, first-time buyers who would have come back don't.

04 / The difference

A template knows the sequence. Not the customer.

Ask AI?

Speed is not strategy. Generic copy misses the objection, flattens the voice, and turns every brand into the same brand. Judgment is the work.

Keep the template?

A template knows the order of the emails. It does not know why this customer hesitated, which proof earns trust, or what makes this product worth returning for.

Hire an agency?

A full team solves a full-team problem. One broken lifecycle flow needs a focused operator, a clear scope, and a direct line from diagnosis to finished copy.
